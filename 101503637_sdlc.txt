The Importance of DevOps in the Software Development Lifecycle

The Software Development Life Cycle, or SDLC, is a process
that teams use to plan, design, build, test, launch, and maintain software.
software applications.

DevOps is important in this process because
it helps development and operations teams work together.

When teams do not communicate well, problems can
come up during software development or release.

DevOps encourages teams to share responsibilities
and work together to deliver reliable software.
Teamwork and Communication

DevOps helps teams communicate better
between software developers and those
who run and maintain it.

Rather than working alone, teams can share updates,
review code, and solve problems as a group.
Tools like GitHub branches and pull requests
help teams work together like this.
This matters even more in group projects where
several students are working on the same repository.
