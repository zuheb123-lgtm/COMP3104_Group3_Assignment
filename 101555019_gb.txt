George Brown College is a public college located in downtown Toronto, Ontario.
It was founded in 1967 and is known for its hands-on, career-focused programs.
I'm currently studying in the Computer Programming and Analysis Programming.

GBC has three main campuses: St. James, Casa Loma, and Waterfront.
St. James is where most business and tech programs are, Casa Loma focuses on trades and technology, and Waterfront is home to the health sciences programs.


GBC offers a wide range of programs including business, design, health sciences, hospitality, construction, and computer technology.
Many programs include co-op or field placements so students get real work experience before graduating.
