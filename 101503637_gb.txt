//////////////George Brown Polytechnic - Own Your Tomorrow//////////////////////////

George Brown Polytechnic  is a public institution in Toronto that focuses on
career-based education and hands-on learning.

What I appreciate about studying at George Brown is that
students have the chance to apply what they learn instead of
just focusing on theory.

As a Computer Programming and Analysis student at the Casa Loma campus,
I work with various programming languages, databases, and tools
that are used in software development.

Through group assignments, I am also learning how important
communication, problem-solving, and teamwork are in the tech field.

These experiences help students get ready for situations they might
face when working in the IT industry.
  
I like the programming labs because I get to practice
what we learned in class instead of just reading about it.

Group projects are an important part of the program
because they teach students how to work with others
and manage deadlines.

By working on these projects, I get experience with tools
like GitHub and learn how software development
works in real-world situations, not just in the classroom.
The slogan "Own Your Tomorrow" connects to my experience because I am 
learning through experience. Own it <3
