George Brown College

George Brown College is a public college in Toronto, Ontario. It was founded in 1967.
Currently offers over 160 career-focused certificate, diploma, postgraduate, and degree programs
across areas like business, culinary arts, design, construction, engineering, and health sciences.

The college emphasizes practical learning through coursework, projects,
and opportunities to apply classroom knowledge. In computer programming,
hands-on assignments help students develop coding, problem-solving,
and teamwork skills.
