Why DevOps Matters in the Software Development Life Cycle

DevOps encourages collaboration between software development and
operations teams. It connects development, testing, deployment, and
maintenance through shared responsibilities and regular feedback.
This helps teams identify problems earlier and deliver improvements
more consistently.

Automation helps teams repeat tasks such as building and testing software
consistently. Continuous integration runs automated checks when changes
are shared, helping the team detect issues before merging. These checks
provide useful feedback, although their effectiveness depends on what
the pipeline actually tests.

In this assignment, separate branches help members work independently,
while pull requests provide a place to review changes before merging.
Clear commit messages make the project history easier to understand
and help the team trace when and why a change was introduced.
